#   Ignoring "The Left" and "The Right" Political Viewpoints

I'm not a big fan of labels (because they very rarely mean what people think they mean) but I think it's important to be clear about my underlying political beliefs.

I'd probably describe myself as a [Liberal Socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_socialism) and a Democrat. i.e. someone who believes in [Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy) which probably makes me a [Democratic Socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_socialism)
but there's also a large amount of [Libertarianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libertarianism) (mostly [Left-Libertarianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left-libertarianism)) and [Anarchism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anarchism) and some [Marxism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxism) mixed in with that.
I also believe in [Free Trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_trade) and the [Free Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_market) for non-essential goods with a massive dose of [Keynesian Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keynesian_economics) laid over the top of that when it comes to the supply & demand needs of a Society.

I know that combination sounds like it should be contradictory and a bit of a mess but it really isn't once you get to the root of each of them.

So some basic principles I believe in are...
- Some kind of organized Society is the best way to deliver the most benefit to the widest number of people.
- There are basic needs that people need to survive and to thrive and that those needs should be met for everyone in Society regardless of their circumstances.
- Some form of Democracy is the best way to manage Society so that the people who are living in that Society have the most say in how that Society is run and how the resources of that Society are distributed.
- Each individual Member of Society has the same Rights & Responsibilities as any other Member of Society.
- People are responsible for their own actions and they should be free to do whatever they want as long as they are not harming other people or the Society in general - if you can't decide whether it will cause harm or not then you probably should not be doing it anyway.
- The Natural Resources belong to Society and should be used for the common benefit of everyone within that Society - I'm strongly **against foreign or private ownership** of Natural Resources

Whether any of these can be considered "Left-wing" or "Right-wing" is a matter of opinion and I don't really care about that. 
I just want to make sure that the Society in which I live is a good one for me and my family and friends and that it is a good one for the majority of people who live in that Society.

My Political Views are the reason why this work exists.
